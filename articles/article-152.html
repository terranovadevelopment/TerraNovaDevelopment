One Human Family: Connected Across Time and Continents

99.9% of our DNA is the same. Our differences are real, but our shared humanity is deeper.

The Human Family

Every human being belongs to one extraordinary family. We speak different languages, inherit different customs, and live in different places, yet our lives are woven together by shared origins and a common future. The message of the illustration is simple: we are connected across time and continents.

What Our DNA Tells Us

Human genomes are commonly described as approximately 99.9% identical when comparing corresponding DNA sequences. This is a useful shorthand, not an exact figure for every pair of people or every type of genetic variation. The small portion that varies helps make each person unique. Genetic differences do not divide humanity into sharply separated biological races.

Our Ancestors Crossed Borders Before Borders Existed

Our ancestors moved, met, formed families, and adapted over thousands of generations. Populations have always had histories of migration and connection. The farther back we trace family trees, the more extensively ancestry overlaps. Modern racial categories do not map neatly onto this complex history. It is more precise to say that we share deep ancestry than to assign everyone the same modern racial identity.

Difference Is Not Division

A shared human origin does not require us to erase culture, heritage, or the experiences of discrimination. Diversity enriches our common story. We can celebrate our different backgrounds while rejecting the idea that ancestry determines anyone’s dignity, intelligence, or worth.

There Is No Them, Only Us

The consequences of this understanding are practical. When a neighbor needs food, housing, healthcare, education, or safety, we are not helping a separate category of people. We are helping members of the same human family. Our institutions and communities should reflect that recognition in how they welcome, protect, and serve everyone.

A Terra Nova Commitment

Terra Nova’s vision begins with a commitment to human dignity. Every person deserves respect and a fair opportunity to thrive. We can build communities that cooperate across differences, share knowledge, solve problems together, and make belonging a reality rather than a slogan.

One Human Family

We are connected by ancestry, by the planet we inhabit, and by the future we are creating together. The hands joined in this illustration represent a choice we can make every day: to recognize one another as family. There is no them. There is only us.

Scientific note: The “99.9%” comparison is an approximate popular summary of human genetic similarity. “Everyone is multiracial” is best understood as a message of shared and overlapping ancestry, not a literal scientific classification of every individual.
