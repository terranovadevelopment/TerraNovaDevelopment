The Jubilee Manifesto

Download Article #112 as Word document

A Call for the Restoration of Humanity, the Renewal of Justice, and the Rebirth of a Sustainable World

I. The Ancient Promise

Across civilizations, there has always been a recognition — sometimes whispered, sometimes declared — that human societies drift toward imbalance. Wealth concentrates. Power hardens. The many labor while the few accumulate. Ancient Israel confronted this truth directly and created a radical institution: the Jubilee.

Every fifty years, debts were wiped clean. Land was restored to families. Bondservants were freed. The economic slate was reset so that no generation could permanently dominate another. Jubilee was not charity. It was not welfare. It was not punishment. It was a structural safeguard against the slow, grinding cruelty of inequality.

It was a recognition that human beings, left to their own devices, will build systems that eventually devour them.

Today, we stand in the shadow of that same truth — but on a global scale.

II. The Modern Catastrophe

Our current economic system is not merely flawed. It is broken beyond repair.

We live in a world where:

  • A tiny fraction of humanity controls more wealth than billions combined.
  • Entire nations drown under public debt that can never be repaid.
  • Private citizens are shackled by mortgages, medical bills, student loans, and credit obligations that follow them like a sentence.
  • Productivity rises while wages stagnate.
  • Housing becomes an investment vehicle instead of a human necessity.
  • The financial sector extracts value without creating it.
  • The cost of living rises faster than the ability to live.

This is not a temporary imbalance. It is not a cyclical downturn. It is not a rough patch.

It is systemic failure — the predictable outcome of a model designed to funnel wealth upward until the bottom collapses.

The polarization is now absolute: a narrow summit of extreme wealth, and a vast plain of struggling humanity.

There is no reform that can fix this. There is no policy tweak that can rebalance it. There is no election that can reverse it.

The system is doing exactly what it was built to do — and that is precisely the problem.

III. The Moral Imperative

Human beings were not created to be debt-serfs. Human societies were not meant to be engines of extraction. Human potential was not meant to be suffocated by interest payments.

A world where the majority live in quiet desperation is not a world worth preserving.

We must confront the truth with courage:

  • The current monetary system is unsustainable.
  • It is mathematically doomed.
  • It is spiritually corrosive.
  • It is morally indefensible.

Debt grows faster than the ability to repay. Wealth grows faster than wages. Automation grows faster than employment. Speculation grows faster than production.

This is not equilibrium. This is collapse in slow motion.

IV. The Jubilee Solution

A modern Jubilee is not a dream. It is not utopian. It is not naïve. It is the only rational response to a system that has reached the end of its lifespan.

1. Forgive All Public Debt

Nations must be freed from the chains of impossible obligations. A country cannot thrive when its future is mortgaged to the past.

2. Forgive All Private Debt

Individuals must be liberated from the burdens that prevent them from living, creating, contributing, and flourishing.

Debt forgiveness is not theft. It is not chaos. It is not irresponsibility. It is resetting the game so humanity can play again.

3. Create a New Economic Structure

After the reset, we must build a system that:

  • Prevents extreme wealth concentration
  • Ensures access to housing, food, healthcare, and education
  • Rewards contribution rather than speculation
  • Protects the planet rather than exploiting it
  • Distributes the bounty of the world among all people

4. Consider a Post-Money Civilization

Money is a tool — not a law of nature. It is a human invention, and like all inventions, it can be retired.

A world without money is not impossible. It is simply unfamiliar.

We already live in a world where digital transactions dominate, where value is abstract, where automation produces abundance. The question is not whether we can build a post-money society.

The question is whether we have the courage to imagine one.

V. The Benefit to Humanity

A global Jubilee would:

  • Restore dignity to billions
  • Unleash creativity and innovation
  • End cycles of poverty that have lasted generations
  • Reduce crime, despair, and social fragmentation
  • Allow nations to invest in their people instead of their creditors
  • Create a foundation for peace rather than competition
  • Rebalance the relationship between humanity and the planet
  • Give every person a genuine chance at life

This is not charity. This is not redistribution. This is not ideology. This is survival — and beyond survival, rebirth.

VI. The Final Truth

We cannot afford to watch another decade pass while an economic system drains opportunity and hope from millions of people. The urgency is not merely personal; it belongs to the world.

We are living in a moment where humanity must choose: continue down a path that leads to collapse, or reset the system and begin again.

A Jubilee is not a fantasy. It is not a relic of ancient scripture. It is a blueprint for survival — and a promise of renewal.

This manifesto is a first step. The next step is to design a transition worthy of its promise.
